### The Benefits of Tiingo's Stock, ETF, and Mutual Fund Split API

#### Current and Future Split Data

Tiingo's Splits API covers splits across Stocks, ETFs, and Mutual Funds - both past and future. With forward split data, you can properly prepare to adjust models for drastic price increases/decreases. Whether you are running research, trading, or running blockchain oracles, knowing corporate actions ahead of time keeps you prepared and ensuring your research runs smoothly.

As new splits are declared, or other splits or cancelled, our API updates to reflect these corporate action changes. You can choose to use our computed split factor, or use your own methodology with our to and from fields.

Check out the Stock, ETF, & Mutual Fund Split API documentation to start querying data now.

#### Precision

The Tiingo Stock, ETF, and Mutual Fund Split API has both the to and from split fields, allowing you to calculate the values as a fraction, or you may use our splitFactor which does this for you as a float. It's your choice as we leave the precision in your hands.
